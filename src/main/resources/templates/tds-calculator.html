TDS Calculator

Calculate your Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) for various payments under different sections.

The Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) Calculator helps you determine the amount of tax to be deducted at source for different types of payments. TDS is a specific amount that is deducted when a payment is made, ensuring that tax is collected in advance from the income of the payee.

Use this calculator to select the nature of payment and the payment amount to estimate the TDS amount. This is applicable for various categories like salaries, interest on securities, dividends, winnings from lotteries, rent payments, and more.
